    How the Hub grows

    The aim is one place where anyone working on hydroclimatic extremes and multi-hazard risk can find the data, the code and the evidence. It grows in stages, and you can help.

    1. Catalogue

      Curated open datasets with access routes and citations.

      Live

    2. Live watch

      Rainfall, river flow and global events from open APIs.

      Live

    3. Community submissions

      Anyone can suggest a dataset through GitHub, reviewed before it is listed.

      Next

    4. Region pages

      Himalaya, Andes and Alps pages with local inventories and agencies.

      Planned

    5. Ask the Hub

      AI search over datasets and literature with cited answers, built like the ICL assistant.

      Planned
