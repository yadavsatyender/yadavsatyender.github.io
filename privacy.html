Privacy and visitor statistics

This site counts visits so I can see which research, datasets and stories are useful. The counting is anonymous and uses no cookies. Your browser keeps only two small choices you make yourself, explained below.

Visitor statistics on this browser: on

What is recorded

  • The page you open, how long you actively read it and how far you scroll.
  • Which sections come into view, which buttons and filters you use, and which links or files you open.
  • The website you came from (its name only, not the full address), and campaign tags if the link had them, such as the QR code on a conference slide.
  • Your country, device type, browser, screen size class and browser language.
  • Page speed measurements and technical errors, so I can fix problems.

What is not recorded

  • No cookies. Your browser stores only two choices you make yourself: if you turn statistics off with the switch above, and if you pick light or dark mode with the sun and moon button in the menu. Both stay on your device and are never sent to this site. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • No IP address. Your address is shortened (the last part removed) and used only to form a code that changes every day and cannot be traced back to you or linked from one day to the next.
  • No city or precise location, and no text you type, including searches in Hazard Hub.
  • No advertising, profiling or tracking across other websites. Nothing is shared or sold.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends either signal, your visit is still counted but without the daily code. To stop all counting, use the switch above.

Where the data is kept, and for how long

The statistics are processed on Cloudflare servers used only for this site and are deleted automatically after 13 months. I am the only person who can see them.
